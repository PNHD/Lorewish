--- Probe 3: RLS blocks a null-owner insert attempt ---
A POSTs a story with owner_user_id explicitly set to null.

PASS — the WITH CHECK clause ((select auth.uid()) = owner_user_id) rejects it before the NOT NULL
column constraint would even be reached; RLS is the first line of defense, not an afterthought.
